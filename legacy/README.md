# Legacy app (out of the build path)

The previous bento/gruvbox portfolio, moved here during the rebuild
([map #1](https://github.com/david-guerra/portfolio/issues/1),
[scaffold ticket #8](https://github.com/david-guerra/portfolio/issues/8)).

Nothing in this folder is built, linted, type-checked, or tested. Its final
deletion belongs to the launch ticket
([#14](https://github.com/david-guerra/portfolio/issues/14)).

The C engines (`c_connect/`, `c_lifegame/`, `c_sudoku/`), the compiled WASM
artifacts (`public/wasm/`), and the workers/hooks were carried forward into the
new app; everything else here is superseded.
