# Contact dialog connection copy

## Context

The current contact copy frames outreach mainly around projects, roles, and problems. The contact surface should also feel open to people who simply want to connect, without becoming overly casual or changing the established visual design.

## Approved copy

- Heading: **Let’s connect.**
- Supporting line: **Projects, ideas, or simply a hello. My inbox is open.**

The existing “Direct line,” “Work email,” and “Elsewhere” labels remain unchanged.

## Scope

This is a copy-only adjustment to the existing contact dialog. Its layout, themes, work-email link, social links, modal behavior, keyboard handling, and reduced-motion behavior remain unchanged.

## Acceptance criteria

- The dialog renders the approved heading and supporting line exactly.
- The copy appears unchanged across dark/light themes and responsive layouts.
- Existing work-email, GitHub, LinkedIn, Escape-close, backdrop-close, inert-background, and focus-restoration behavior continues to pass.
- No contact form or third-party contact provider is reintroduced.
